# Support Our Initiatives

KOINOVA exists to open doors for people. Your support helps more people walk through them.

## Ways to support
- **Sponsor an event or programme** — help us host training, challenges and gatherings.
- **Fund scholarships or fellowships** — make learning and opportunity accessible. [AVAILABLE ONLY WHEN PROGRAMMES EXIST]
- **Give** — support our general work. [DONATION LINK, ONLY WHEN THE ENTITY AND PAYMENT SETUP ARE APPROVED]
- **Offer in-kind support** — venues, cloud credits, software, equipment, printing or travel.
- **Give your expertise** — mentor, teach, judge a challenge or review a publication.
- **Spread the word** — introduce KOINOVA to people and organisations who could help.

## How we use support
We will state clearly what each form of support funds and report on how it was used. [CONFIRM REPORTING COMMITMENT]

## Our commitments
- Honest communication about what we do and do not yet do
- Responsible handling of funds, data and goodwill
- Respect for donor wishes where they are agreed in writing
- No pressure and no misleading appeals
