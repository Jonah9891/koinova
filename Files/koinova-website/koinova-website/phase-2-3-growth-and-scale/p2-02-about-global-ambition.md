# Our global ambition

Faith-driven talent exists in every region. Opportunity does not. KOINOVA is being built to close that gap.

## What we are aiming for
To become a recognised platform where faith-driven talents, ideas and innovations connect with opportunities across the world.

## What that looks like
- **A connected community** of Christian technologists, founders, researchers and students in many countries.
- **Regional chapters** led by local coordinators who understand their own context.
- **Partnerships** with universities, innovation hubs, companies, churches and institutions.
- **Shared knowledge** through training, research and open resources.
- **Gatherings** that bring people together, online and in person.
- **Economic opportunity** through jobs, funding, fellowships and entrepreneurship.

## How we expect to grow
We will grow in stages, and only as fast as we can serve people well.

1. **Foundation** — build the community, programmes and governance.
2. **Connect** — link members and partners across countries.
3. **Expand** — open chapters and regional programmes.
4. **Lead** — host flagship gatherings and contribute to wider conversations on faith and technology.
