# Startup Programmes

Support for Christian founders building sustainable, impactful, technology-driven businesses. Part of KOINOVA Ventures.

## The path
1. **Ideate** — sharpen the problem you want to solve.
2. **Validate** — test the idea with real people before you build.
3. **Build** — shape the product, business model and team.
4. **Launch** — get to your first customers.
5. **Grow** — build systems, funding readiness and partnerships.

## What founders can expect
- Mentorship from experienced founders and professionals
- Workshops on strategy, product, branding, sales and finance
- Guidance on business models and startup readiness
- Introductions to partners and investors where there is a good fit
- A community of fellow founders
