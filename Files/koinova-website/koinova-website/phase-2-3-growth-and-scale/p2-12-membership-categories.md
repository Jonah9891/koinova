# Membership

KOINOVA membership is open to Christians who share our biblical foundation and a commitment to faith-driven innovation. Choose the category that fits you best.

## Categories

### Student and emerging talent
For students and people starting out. Learn, find mentors, discover opportunities and build early connections.

### Technology professional
For developers, engineers, designers, cybersecurity, data and AI specialists. Network, grow your career, collaborate and give back.

### Entrepreneur and founder
For people building or planning ventures. Access founder support, peers, mentors and routes to partners and investors.

### Researcher and academic
For researchers and educators. Collaborate on projects, publish and connect with practitioners.

### Technology leader and executive
For senior professionals. Mentor, advise, shape programmes and connect across sectors.

### Organisation and institution
For companies, universities, churches, hubs and nonprofits. Partner, sponsor and take part in programmes. See **Partner With Us** and **Institutional Partners**.
