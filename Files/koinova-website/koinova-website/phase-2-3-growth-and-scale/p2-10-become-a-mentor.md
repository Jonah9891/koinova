# Become a Mentor

You have learned things the hard way. Help someone learn them sooner.

## Who we are looking for
Experienced professionals, founders, researchers and leaders in technology, business and related fields who are committed to Christian values and to developing others.

## What mentors do
- Meet a mentee regularly [FREQUENCY AND DURATION]
- Listen, ask good questions and share experience
- Offer honest, constructive guidance
- Respect confidentiality and boundaries
- Help mentees set and review goals

## What you will gain
- The satisfaction of investing in someone's growth
- A network of experienced peers
- Recognition within the KOINOVA community
- Opportunities to speak, teach and contribute

## How we select mentors
1. You complete the application below.
2. We review it and may arrange a short conversation.
3. You agree to our Code of Conduct and Safeguarding rules. [CONFIRM ANY BACKGROUND-CHECK POLICY]
4. We match you with a mentee based on goals and availability.

## Application
**Fields:** Full name · Email · Country · Current role and organisation · Years of experience · Areas you can mentor in (multi-select) · LinkedIn or portfolio · Why you want to mentor · Hours per month available · Confirm you agree to the Code of Conduct · Privacy consent

**Success message:** Thank you. We will review your application and be in touch.
