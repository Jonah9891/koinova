# Governance and accountability

KOINOVA asks people to trust us with their time, data and ambitions. Good governance is how we earn and keep that trust.

## Our principles
- **Integrity first.** We act honestly and say what is true, including what we have not yet done.
- **Transparency.** We publish how we are structured, how decisions are made and how resources are used.
- **Accountability.** Leaders answer to the community and to the law.
- **Stewardship.** We use money, data and goodwill carefully and for their intended purpose.
- **Safeguarding.** We protect members, especially young people and vulnerable people, in every programme.

## How KOINOVA is structured
