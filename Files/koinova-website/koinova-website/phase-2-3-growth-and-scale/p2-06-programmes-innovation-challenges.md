# Innovation Challenges

Real problems. Real teams. Practical solutions. Part of KOINOVA Innovation.

## How a challenge works
1. **Brief** — we publish a problem to solve and the rules.
2. **Team up** — join with others or enter solo.
3. **Build** — work on your solution with access to mentors.
4. **Showcase** — present your work to a panel and the community.
5. **Feedback** — receive comments and, where offered, recognition or support.
