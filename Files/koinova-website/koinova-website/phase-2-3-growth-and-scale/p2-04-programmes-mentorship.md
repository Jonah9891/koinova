# Mentorship

Growth is faster when someone further along the road walks with you.

## How it works
1. **Apply** — tell us your goals, background and the kind of support you want.
2. **Get matched** — we pair you using your goals, field and availability.
3. **Meet** — you and your mentor meet regularly, online or in person.
4. **Review** — at the end of the period you reflect on progress and decide what next.

## For mentees
Get guidance on career decisions, skills, ventures, leadership and balancing faith with work.

## For mentors
Share your experience, help someone grow and strengthen the next generation of Christian innovators. See **Become a Mentor**.
