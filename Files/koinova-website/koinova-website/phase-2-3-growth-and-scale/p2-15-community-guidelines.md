# Community Guidelines

KOINOVA is a community of people from many countries and church backgrounds. These guidelines help us keep it respectful, safe and useful.

## We expect members to
- **Be respectful.** Treat everyone with dignity. Disagree kindly.
- **Be honest.** Do not misrepresent who you are, your qualifications or your work.
- **Be safe.** Protect your own and others' privacy. Never share someone's personal details without consent.
- **Be constructive.** Help, encourage and share knowledge.
- **Be professional.** Keep messages relevant. No spam, selling, recruiting without permission or unsolicited promotion.
- **Honour confidentiality.** What is shared in mentorship or private conversations stays there.

## We do not allow
- Harassment, abuse, hate speech or discrimination
- Threats or intimidation
- Sexual or inappropriate content or contact
- Scams, fraud, pyramid or get-rich-quick schemes
- Impersonation
- Sharing illegal or harmful content
- Contacting minors in ways that breach our safeguarding rules

## Reporting
If you see something that breaks these guidelines, use **Report** on the profile or message, or email [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What happens next
We review reports fairly and promptly. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, suspension or removal from KOINOVA. Serious matters may be referred to the authorities.

## Disagreement and faith
We welcome people from different denominations. Share your faith with respect. Do not attack others' beliefs or use KOINOVA to pressure people.
