# Training & Masterclasses

Practical learning for people who want to build, lead and grow. Part of KOINOVA Academy.

## What you can learn
- **Technology and digital skills** — the foundations employers and founders need.
- **AI and responsible AI** — how to use and build with AI wisely and ethically.
- **Entrepreneurship** — from idea to first customers.
- **Career development** — CVs, interviews, portfolios and professional growth.
- **Leadership** — leading teams with integrity and purpose.

## How it works
- **Live masterclasses** led by practitioners, with time for questions.
- **Workshops** where you build something hands-on.
- **Self-paced learning** you can complete in your own time.
- **Cohorts** for deeper programmes, with mentors and peers.

## Who it is for
Students, early-career professionals, career changers, founders and leaders. Levels are shown on every course.
