# Chapters

A global community needs local roots.

## What a chapter is
A local group of KOINOVA members who meet, learn and build together in a city, country or region, guided by a coordinator and supported by the wider network.

## Start a chapter
**What it takes**
- A group of at least [NUMBER] interested members
- A coordinator who agrees to our Chapter Guidelines
- A plan for a first activity
- Alignment with our Statement of Faith and values

**What you receive**
Branding and templates · Event and communication guidance · Introductions to partners · A place in the global network

**Steps**
1. Express interest.
2. Speak with our team.
3. Gather your first members.
4. Plan and hold your first activity.
5. Be confirmed as a chapter.

**Form fields:** Name · Email · City and country · Why you want to start a chapter · Who you will work with · Experience organising groups
