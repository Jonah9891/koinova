# 04-BRAND-AND-VOICE-GUIDE.md

## 1. Brand essentials
- **Name:** KOINOVA (always capitalised)
- **Tagline:** Where Faith Meets Innovation.
- **Supporting line:** Faith-Inspired. Innovation-Driven. Impact-Focused.
- **Name meaning:** *Koinonia* (fellowship, shared participation) + *Nova* (newness, innovation).
- **Ecosystem names:** KOINOVA Community, Academy, Innovation, Ventures, Global.

## 2. Logo usage
- Logo supplied by the human. Store in `src/assets/brand/`.
- Provide and use: full logo (light theme), full logo (dark theme), icon-only mark, favicon set, social share image.
- Clear space around the logo equals the height of the icon mark.
- Do not stretch, recolour, add effects or place on busy backgrounds.
- If only one logo variant exists, flag it in `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` and use a CSS-safe backing panel in the other theme until a variant arrives.

## 3. Personality
Visionary · Intelligent · Innovative · Professional · Faith-centred · Collaborative · Impact-oriented · Forward-thinking.

## 4. Voice principles
| Principle | In practice |
|---|---|
| Professional and credible | Precise language, no hype, no unverified claims |
| Clear and accessible | Short sentences, plain words, explain jargon |
| Inspirational without exaggeration | Vision stated confidently, never "world's best" |
| Innovative and forward-thinking | Active verbs: build, create, connect, equip |
| Grounded in biblical values | Faith is stated openly, not forced into every line |
| Globally relevant | Avoid culture-specific idioms and slang |
| Inclusive across denominations | Avoid denominational jargon; say "Christians" and "biblical principles" |
| Practical and impact-focused | Lead with what people can do |

## 5. Writing rules
- British English (organisation, programme, mobilise).
- Sentence case for headings (not Title Case), except proper names.
- One idea per paragraph. Paragraphs of 1 to 3 sentences on web pages.
- Use "we" for KOINOVA and "you" for the visitor.
- Buttons: verb first and short (Join KOINOVA, Explore the ecosystem, Send message).
- Never invent numbers, testimonials, partners or events.
- Be honest about stage: "founding community", "launching progressively".

## 6. Words to prefer and avoid
| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| build, create, equip, connect, empower | revolutionise, disrupt, game-changing |
| founding community | thousands of members (unless true) |
| launching progressively | coming soon (when vague), "stay tuned" |
| biblical principles, Kingdom values | culture-war language, denominational labels |
| solutions to real-world problems | "cutting-edge", "next-generation" filler |

## 7. Microcopy standards
- **Success:** warm and specific. "Welcome to KOINOVA. We are glad you are here."
- **Errors:** say what went wrong and what to do. "That email does not look right. Please check it and try again."
- **Empty states:** honest and inviting. "Our first events are being planned. Subscribe to be the first to hear."
- **Loading:** short and calm. "Sending..."
- **Confirmations by email:** plain text first, branded HTML second, always with an unsubscribe or contact line.

## 8. Imagery direction
Full rules are in `19-IMAGERY-AND-MOCKUPS-GUIDE.md`. Summary:
- Real photographs of real people only, with a balanced mix of Black African and white people, different ages and genders, in professional technology and entrepreneurship settings.
- Client images first, then licensed stock, then code-built mockups, then gradients and SVG.
- No AI-generated images. No stock clichés (handshakes, glowing brains, praying silhouettes over circuit boards).
- Faith expressed subtly: light, community, craftsmanship, building.
- Gradients and abstract visuals follow the one-family rule in `05-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`.
- Every image needs alt text, a recorded licence and an optimised file.

## 9. Iconography
- Single consistent line-icon set (for example Lucide or Phosphor, inlined as SVG sprite). 24px grid, 1.5px stroke.
- Ecosystem icons: Community (network nodes), Academy (book or graduation), Innovation (spark or lightbulb), Ventures (rocket or sprout), Global (globe).

## 10. Scripture and faith content
- Scripture referenced by book and chapter in the copy; quote only from a chosen, licensed translation.
- Tone: respectful, confident and invitational. Not judgemental or exclusionary.

## 11. Social and sharing
- Open Graph image: logo, tagline, brand background, 1200 x 630.
- Default share text: "KOINOVA — Where Faith Meets Innovation. A global Christian technology and innovation ecosystem."
