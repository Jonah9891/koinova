# 00-PROJECT-BRIEF.md

## Project
KOINOVA website and member sign-up web app.

## One-line summary
A modern, animated, accessible website (with dark and light modes) that introduces KOINOVA and turns visitors into founding members.

## About KOINOVA
KOINOVA is a global Christian technology and innovation ecosystem committed to connecting, equipping and empowering Christians to contribute to technology, entrepreneurship and innovation. It is interdenominational, hybrid (community, education, innovation, ventures, partnerships) and global in ambition.

**Tagline:** Where Faith Meets Innovation.
**Supporting line:** Faith-Inspired. Innovation-Driven. Impact-Focused.
**Ecosystem:** Community · Academy · Innovation · Ventures · Global.
**Eight pillars:** Technology & Digital Transformation · AI & Emerging Technologies · Entrepreneurship & Startups · Innovation & Research · Career & Professional Development · Education & Capacity Development · Kingdom Influence & Leadership · Partnerships & Global Collaboration.

## Goals
1. **Primary:** convert visitors into founding members through the JOIN KOINOVA sign-up.
2. Communicate credibility, vision and faith clearly within the first 10 seconds.
3. Attract partners, mentors, volunteers and investors.
4. Build an email list for launch announcements.
5. Create a foundation that can grow into a member platform (login, profiles, directory) without a rebuild.

## Audience
- Technology professionals (developers, engineers, designers, cybersecurity, data and AI specialists)
- Entrepreneurs and startup founders
- Students and emerging talents
- Researchers and academics
- Technology leaders and executives
- Investors and strategic partners
- Churches and Christian organisations

Visitors come from many countries. Many will browse on mobile and on slower connections.

## Brand personality
Visionary, intelligent, innovative, professional, faith-centred, collaborative, impact-oriented, forward-thinking.

**Look and feel:** modern, clean, confident, premium. Not preachy, not corporate-cold, not clip-art "church website". Technology-forward with warmth.

## Reference sites
Use for structure, spacing, tone and inspiration only. Do not copy layouts, text or assets.
- https://www.qubators.org — a global network of Christian developers, designers and entrepreneurs. Study: how it communicates a global network and invites people to join a local hub.
- https://www.christiantechcommunity.org — a Christian technology community. Study: how it presents community, resources and calls to action.

> NOTE: Both sites render with JavaScript. The human may supply screenshots and notes about what they like. Record anything learned in `DECISIONS.md`.

## Brand assets
- Logo: supplied by the human. Place in `/assets/brand/`. Expected: SVG (preferred), plus light-on-dark and dark-on-light variants if available.
- Colours (final): Deep Navy Blue `#101C3A` (primary), Emerald Green `#0D9B72` (secondary), Champagne Gold `#D6AD60` (accent), Innovation Blue `#2563EB` (supporting), White `#FFFFFF` and Soft Grey `#F3F5F8` (neutrals). See `05-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`.
- Fonts: to be confirmed. Recommended defaults are in the design system.
- If any asset is missing, build with a clearly marked temporary placeholder and log it in `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.

## Success measures (first 90 days after launch)
- Member sign-up conversion rate (visitors who complete the form)
- Total founding members and countries represented
- Newsletter subscribers
- Partner and volunteer enquiries received
- Core Web Vitals in the "good" range
- Lighthouse 90+ across all categories

> Set targets once real traffic data exists.

## Constraints
- Stack: HTML, CSS, JavaScript. Hosting: Vercel (to start).
- Budget: free tiers where possible (Vercel, Supabase, Resend).
- Membership is free at launch.
- Launch date and team: to be decided.

Professional and credible. Clear and accessible. Inspirational without exaggeration. Grounded in biblical values. Globally relevant. Inclusive across denominations. Focused on practical solutions and impact. Full guide in `04-BRAND-AND-VOICE-GUIDE.md`.
