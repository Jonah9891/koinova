# Vision & Mission

## Our Vision

To inspire and empower a generation of Christian innovators, entrepreneurs and technology leaders who shape the future through faith, innovation and transformative solutions.

## Our Mission

To build a global ecosystem that connects, equips and empowers Christians to leverage technology, entrepreneurship and innovation to solve real-world problems, create opportunities and make meaningful contributions to society, while upholding biblical principles and Kingdom values.

## Our Purpose

To mobilise and empower Christians to become active contributors to technological advancement, innovation and entrepreneurship, using their knowledge, creativity, talents and resources to develop solutions that positively transform lives and communities.

## Our Promise

To create an enabling and collaborative ecosystem where Christians can connect, grow, innovate, access opportunities and contribute meaningfully to the advancement of technology and society, in an environment shaped by faith, excellence, integrity, knowledge-sharing, collaboration and purposeful innovation.

## How we intend to make a difference

Our approach follows a simple pathway:

1. **Inputs** — people, knowledge, technology, expertise, partnerships and resources.
2. **Activities** — community, training, mentorship, research, innovation programmes and entrepreneurship support.
3. **Outputs** — trained individuals, professional connections, developed ideas, collaborative projects and startup initiatives.
4. **Outcomes** — stronger professional capability, entrepreneurial capacity, collaboration and opportunity.
5. **Long-term impact** — a globally connected generation of Christian innovators contributing to technological advancement, economic development and positive change.

This is our intended pathway. We will assess our actual results through measurable programme outcomes and report on them honestly.
