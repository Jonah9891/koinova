# Volunteer With Us

KOINOVA is being built by people who care about its mission. If you would like to contribute your time and skills, we would like to hear from you.

## Where we need help

- Technology and website development
- Design and branding
- Content writing and editing
- Social media and communications
- Event planning and hosting
- Research and community building
- Partnerships and outreach
- Mentorship and advisory support
- Leadership and governance support

## What to expect

Volunteer roles are shaped around your skills and availability. We will be clear about what is expected and will recognise your contribution.
