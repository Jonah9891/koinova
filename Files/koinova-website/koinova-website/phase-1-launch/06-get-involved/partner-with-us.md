# Partner With Us

KOINOVA believes that meaningful progress happens through collaboration. We are open to partnerships that expand access to knowledge, resources and opportunities for our community.

## Who we want to work with

- Technology companies
- Universities and educational institutions
- Innovation hubs and startup ecosystems
- Investors and venture capital organisations
- Churches and Christian organisations
- Professional associations
- Research institutions
- Development organisations
- Corporate organisations

## Ways to partner

- **Programme partnerships** — co-delivering training, challenges or events
- **Sponsorship** — supporting events and initiatives
- **Learning and talent partnerships** — scholarships, internships, mentorship
- **Research collaboration** — joint projects and knowledge exchange
- **Investment and ventures** — supporting founders and startups
- **Community partnerships** — churches and ministries engaging their members in technology and innovation
- **Institutional membership** — organisational participation (details coming)

## What we offer partners

Access to a growing community of faith-driven professionals and founders, visibility in programmes aligned with their values, and a trusted route into a global Christian technology network.
