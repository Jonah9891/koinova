# Join KOINOVA

**Headline:** Be part of the founding community.

KOINOVA is just beginning, and the people who join now will help shape what it becomes. Register to connect with others, hear about programmes and opportunities first, and contribute to a global ecosystem of Christian innovators.

## Who can join

Christians from any denomination who share our biblical foundation and a commitment to faith-driven innovation:

- Technology professionals
- Entrepreneurs and founders
- Students and emerging talents
- Researchers and academics
- Technology leaders and executives
- Strategic and institutional partners

## What you can expect

- Updates on programmes, events and opportunities
- Access to our growing community as it opens
- A voice in shaping KOINOVA
- Early access to the Academy, Innovation and Ventures programmes
