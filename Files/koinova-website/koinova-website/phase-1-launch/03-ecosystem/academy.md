# KOINOVA Academy

**Tagline:** Learn. Build. Lead.

## Equipping Christians for the future of work

Technology skills, digital confidence and entrepreneurial thinking are among the most valuable assets today. KOINOVA Academy is being developed to give Christians access to relevant knowledge and practical learning.

## What the Academy is planned to include

- Technology and digital skills training
- AI education and responsible AI
- Entrepreneurship programmes
- Professional and career development
- Leadership training
- Masterclasses and workshops
- Online learning resources

## Our approach to learning

Practical over theoretical, accessible to different levels, rooted in integrity and excellence, and focused on applying skills to real problems.

## Status

Launching progressively. Register your interest and we will notify you when the first courses and masterclasses open.
