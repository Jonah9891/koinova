# KOINOVA Global

**Tagline:** Connected across borders.

## Innovation has no borders

Christian technologists and entrepreneurs are working in every region of the world, often without connection to each other. KOINOVA Global is our international collaboration and partnership network.

## What we aim to build

- International partnerships
- Global conferences and gatherings
- Collaborations with institutions and universities
- Cross-border networking
- International knowledge exchange
- Strategic alliances
- Global community development

## Our global ambition

To become a recognised platform through which faith-driven talents, ideas and innovations connect with opportunities across the world.

## Who we want to work with

Technology companies, universities, innovation hubs, startup ecosystems, investors, churches, Christian organisations, professional associations, research institutions, development organisations and corporates.

## Status

Launching progressively. If you represent an organisation or want to help start something in your country, get in touch.
