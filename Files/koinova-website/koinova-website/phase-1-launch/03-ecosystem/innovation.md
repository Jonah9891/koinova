# KOINOVA Innovation

**Tagline:** Imagine. Experiment. Solve.

## Turning ideas into solutions

Good ideas are not enough. They need space, collaboration and support to become something useful. KOINOVA Innovation is an environment for exploring ideas, testing them and developing them into practical solutions.

## What we aim to support

- Innovation challenges
- Applied research and research collaboration
- Product development and prototypes
- Technology projects with collaborators
- Innovation showcases

## Innovation guided by wisdom

We believe innovation should be guided by wisdom, responsibility, ethical consideration and respect for human dignity. This is especially important in AI and other emerging technologies.

## Status

Launching progressively. Members will be the first to hear about challenges and collaboration opportunities.
