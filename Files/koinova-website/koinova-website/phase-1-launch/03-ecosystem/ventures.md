# KOINOVA Ventures

**Tagline:** Build. Grow. Create value.

## Entrepreneurship that serves

Entrepreneurship creates value, solves problems and generates employment. KOINOVA Ventures is being developed to support Christian founders building sustainable, impactful, technology-driven businesses.

## What we aim to offer

- Founder mentorship
- Startup development and venture-building programmes
- Business model and product development guidance
- Startup readiness support
- Connections to investors and partners
- Entrepreneurial networking

## Who it is for

Aspiring entrepreneurs, early-stage founders, business owners adopting technology, and investors interested in supporting purpose-driven ventures.

## Status

Launching progressively. Founders and investors can register interest now to help shape the programme.
