## Frequently asked questions

**What is KOINOVA?**
A global Christian technology and innovation ecosystem connecting, equipping and empowering Christians to contribute to technology, entrepreneurship and innovation.

**Who can join?**
Christians from any denomination who share our biblical foundation and a commitment to faith-driven innovation, including professionals, founders, students, researchers, leaders and organisations.

**Is KOINOVA a church?**
No. KOINOVA is a technology and innovation ecosystem grounded in Christian faith. We work with churches and Christian organisations as partners.

**Do I need to work in technology?**
No. If you are interested in technology, innovation or entrepreneurship, you are welcome.
