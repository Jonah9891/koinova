# KOINOVA Website Content Pack

Where Faith Meets Innovation.
Faith-Inspired. Innovation-Driven. Impact-Focused.

This pack contains the written content for the full KOINOVA website, organised by release phase so everything can be uploaded in one go and published in stages.

## Folder structure

- `phase-1-launch/` — pages to publish at launch (25 files)
- `phase-2-3-growth-and-scale/` — copy for Phase 2 (`p2-` files) and Phase 3 (`p3-` files) in one folder, each with a `publish_when` condition

## How each page file is laid out

Every file begins with a short header block:

- `page` — page title
- `url` — suggested URL slug
- `menu` — where it sits in the navigation
- `seo_title` — browser/search title (under 60 characters)
- `meta_description` — search snippet (under 160 characters)
- `phase` — 1, 2 or 3

Below the header is the page copy, with suggested section headings, buttons (shown as **[BUTTON TEXT]**) and developer notes (shown as `> DEV NOTE:`).

## Placeholders to fill in before publishing

Anything in square brackets in capitals needs your real details. Nothing has been invented on your behalf.

- `[CONTACT EMAIL]`, `[PARTNERSHIPS EMAIL]`, `[PRIVACY EMAIL]`
- `[ROLE AT KOINOVA]`, `[LINKEDIN]`, `[WEBSITE]`, `[NETICOX HUB URL]`, `[FOUNDA360 URL]`, `[AFRINGO NETWORK URL]`
- `[LAUNCH DATE]`, `[FOUNDER NAME]`, `[LEADERSHIP NAMES AND BIOS]`
- `[MEMBERSHIP FORM LINK]`, `[VOLUNTEER FORM LINK]`, `[PARTNER FORM LINK]`
- `[SOCIAL LINKS]`, `[REGISTERED ENTITY NAME AND COUNTRY]`

## Important notes

1. **Honest launch copy.** Phase 1 pages describe plans and intentions ("we are building", "launching soon") rather than claiming results, members or partners you do not yet have. Update the wording as things become real.
2. **Legal pages are drafts.** The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Cookie Policy are starting templates. Have a lawyer review them for your registered country and your users' countries before publishing.
3. **Scripture.** Verses are referenced by book and chapter, not quoted, so you can choose your preferred Bible translation and check its licensing.
4. **Phase rollout.** Only publish a page when its programme, content or feature actually exists. Hidden "coming soon" pages hurt credibility more than missing ones.
