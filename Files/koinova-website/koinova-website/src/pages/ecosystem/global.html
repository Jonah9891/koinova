Connected across borders.

Innovation has no borders. Christian technologists and entrepreneurs are working in every region of the world, often without connection to each other. KOINOVA Global is our international collaboration and partnership network.

International partnerships

In-person and virtual summits uniting Christian tech builders.

Joint research, student development, and academic exchanges.

Global peer groups sharing regional technology trends.

Strategic alliances

Connecting Kingdom capital, venture talent, and emerging markets.

Our global ambition

To become a recognised platform through which faith-driven talents, ideas and innovations connect with opportunities across the world.

Who we want to work with

Technology companies, universities, innovation hubs, startup ecosystems, investors, churches, Christian organisations, professional associations, research institutions, development organisations and corporates.
