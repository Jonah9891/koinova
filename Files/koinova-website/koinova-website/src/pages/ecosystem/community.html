KOINOVA Community

Connect. Collaborate. Grow.

A community where faith and technology meet. Many Christians work in technology, business and research without knowing others who share their faith and their field. KOINOVA Community exists to change that.

What the Community is designed to offer

Professional networking

Connect with like-minded Christians across disciplines and countries.

Collaboration on ideas and projects

Build technical initiatives and products with shared Kingdom ethics.

Knowledge-sharing and peer support

Exchange technical insights, design patterns, and engineering wisdom.

Mentorship and guidance

Learn from seasoned executives and mentor emerging young builders.

Discovery of opportunities

Access vetted career, research, grant, and fellowship openings.

Who it is for

Software developers, engineers, designers, cybersecurity professionals, data analysts, AI specialists, founders, students, researchers, executives and anyone building a career in technology and innovation.

Our community culture

Continuous learning, ethical leadership, collaboration, mentorship, service and respect across Christian denominations and professional backgrounds.
