Guiding Convictions

Our Eight Core Values

Faith

We uphold biblical principles as the foundation of our identity, decisions, relationships and activities.

Excellence

We pursue quality, professionalism and continuous improvement in everything we do.

Innovation

We embrace creativity, critical thinking, experimentation and emerging technologies, and encourage practical approaches to solving problems.

Integrity

We uphold honesty, transparency, accountability and ethical conduct.

Collaboration

We believe meaningful progress happens when people share knowledge, resources, experiences and opportunities.

Stewardship

Knowledge, talents, opportunities, technology and resources come with responsibility. We commit to using them wisely and purposefully.

Service

Our abilities and innovations should improve lives, solve problems and create value for others.

Impact

We measure progress by the positive difference our work creates in society.

Our Philosophy

Guiding Principles for Tech & Faith

  • • Faith and technology are not opposing forces.
  • • Christians have a responsibility to contribute to technology, entrepreneurship and innovation, not only to consume them.
  • • Innovation should be guided by wisdom, responsibility, ethics and respect for human dignity.
  • • Entrepreneurship creates value, solves problems and generates employment.
  • • Knowledge should be shared, opportunities created and collaboration encouraged.
  • • Above all, our faith should influence how we think, create, lead, innovate and serve.
Christian Foundations

Statement of Faith

KOINOVA is founded on the Christian faith and recognises the Bible as the foundation of its values and principles. We believe in:

  • The sovereignty of God and His purpose for humanity.
  • The authority of the Holy Bible as God's Word and a guide for faith and conduct.
  • Jesus Christ as Lord and Saviour.
  • The importance of Christian character, integrity and responsible living.
  • The value of human life, dignity and God-given potential.
  • The responsibility to use our talents, knowledge and resources purposefully.
  • The importance of excellence, stewardship, service and ethical leadership.
  • The opportunity to demonstrate Christian values through professional conduct, entrepreneurship, innovation and service to society.

An interdenominational community

KOINOVA welcomes Christians from different denominations and church backgrounds who share a commitment to biblical principles and the responsible advancement of technology and innovation. Our approach is collaborative and respectful of the diversity within the wider Christian community.
