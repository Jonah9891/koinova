Featured Leader

Dansu Semawon Jonah

Business Transformation Strategist | Venture Builder | Brand Strategist | Technology Entrepreneur

Dansu Semawon Jonah is a business transformation strategist, venture builder, brand strategist and technology-driven entrepreneur with a strong interest in business development, innovation, digital transformation and entrepreneurship. His work focuses on helping individuals, businesses and organisations turn ideas into viable ventures, overcome growth challenges and build sustainable, competitive solutions.

With expertise spanning venture building, business strategy, business development, digital marketing, branding, sales, technology and artificial intelligence, he works at the intersection of business and technology to identify opportunities, develop strategies and drive meaningful transformation.

A committed Christian, Dansu believes that knowledge, skills, creativity and opportunities should be guided by faith, integrity, excellence and a sense of purpose. He is particularly passionate about applying his expertise to solve real-world problems, create economic opportunities, empower people and contribute to sustainable development.

He is the author of six published books and an alumnus of the Enterprise Development Centre (EDC), Lagos, and the Tony Elumelu Foundation (TEF). His professional development also includes training from institutions such as IIDE India and ALX, alongside other learning platforms.

Dansu is the Founder of NETICOX HUB LTD, a technology and innovation company focused on supporting entrepreneurs, businesses and social enterprises through technology, strategy, branding, digital transformation and business development.

He is also driving the development of FOUNDA360, the company's emerging venture studio brand, established around the vision of transforming ideas and business opportunities into scalable, technology-enabled ventures.

In the social impact space, he founded NETICOX AfriNGO Network, an initiative focused on strengthening Africa's nonprofit sector through organisational development, technology adoption, visibility and access to relevant opportunities.

Beyond his entrepreneurial pursuits, Dansu is an advocate for youth empowerment, digital skills development, innovation and sustainable development. His broader vision is to contribute to building a more innovative and economically competitive Africa by empowering people, strengthening businesses and developing solutions that address pressing social and economic challenges.

His work is guided by a commitment to faith, purposeful leadership, continuous learning, excellence and meaningful impact across Africa and beyond.
